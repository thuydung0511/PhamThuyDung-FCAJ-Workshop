Below is my personal feedback on participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam**, to help the FCAJ team improve future cohort experiences.

### Overall evaluation

**1. Working environment**

The FCAJ program provides an open, supportive environment. During the sessions at the AWS Hanoi office, mentors are approachable when technical or administrative questions arise, and the sessions create a comfortable space to learn AWS services.

**2. Support from mentor / team admin**

My mentor guided me during the sessions at the AWS Hanoi office and encouraged me to attempt solutions independently before asking for answers. The admin team supported onboarding, documentation, and program logistics so I could focus on project delivery.

**3. Relevance of work to academic major**

The capstone CloudNote project aligned well with my **Computer Science** studies while extending into areas not fully covered in coursework — serverless design, DynamoDB data modeling, API Gateway integration, CloudWatch monitoring, and CloudTrail auditing. The work bridged university theory with production-style AWS practice.

**4. Learning & skill development opportunities**

Beyond the capstone, I gained practical skills through the sessions at the AWS Hanoi office, workshop labs, Hugo report publishing, and fixing deployment errors on GitHub Pages. These experiences improved both technical depth and professional reporting habits.

**5. Company culture & team spirit**

The program culture emphasizes learning by doing and documenting the work. During the sessions at the AWS Hanoi office, I felt comfortable asking questions and was encouraged to document and present my work.

**6. Internship policies / benefits**

The structured 5-week timeline, clear deliverables (worklog, proposal, workshop, report), and access to AWS learning resources provided a well-defined internship path. The sessions at the AWS Hanoi office were a valuable complement to self-directed project work.

---

### Additional reflections

**What I found most satisfying:** Building end-to-end serverless infrastructure for CloudNote — from DynamoDB and Lambda through API Gateway, S3 static hosting, Cognito sign-in, the "Read note" feature with Amazon Polly, CloudWatch dashboards and CloudTrail — and seeing the full-stack notes application work live in the browser.

**What could be improved for future interns:** I did the capstone on my own, so I had to manage my time myself. Interns working alone would benefit from setting hands-on milestones early instead of leaving most of the practice to the final weeks.

**Would I recommend this program?** Yes. FCAJ offers mentorship, real AWS project experience, and in-person sessions at the AWS Hanoi office — suitable for students who want hands-on cloud engineering beyond classroom labs.

---

### Suggestions & expectations

I suggest maintaining the balance between self-paced lab work and the in-person sessions at the AWS Hanoi office, where interns can ask mentors directly.

I would be interested in staying connected with the FCAJ / AWS study community after the program.

**Other comments:** Thank you to the mentors and organizers for a meaningful internship experience. The combination of project work, the sessions at the AWS Hanoi office, and report documentation helped me grow both technically and professionally.
